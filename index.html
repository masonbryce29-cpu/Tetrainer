HOW TO PLAY

BASICS & CONTROLS

Use the Left / Right Arrows to move pieces side to side.

Use the Up Arrow to rotate Clockwise and Z to rotate Counter-Clockwise.

Press the Down Arrow to Soft Drop pieces faster.

Press Spacebar to Hard Drop a piece instantly.

Press C to Hold a piece for later use.

(Note: All controls can be changed in the KEYBINDS menu!)

SCORING & COMBOS

Fill an entire row with blocks to clear it. Clearing multiple lines at once earns you more points and sends Garbage Lines to your opponent!

Chain line clears together without dropping a piece that doesn't clear a line to build a COMBO multiplier for huge bonus damage.

SPIN MECHANICS

When a piece is twisted into a tight spot right before locking, it performs a SPIN.
These deal extreme damage, equal to clearing double the lines!

BACK-TO-BACK (B2B)

Perform a Spin or Quad, followed by another Spin or Quad, to build a B2B chain. Dropping a normal piece will RELEASE your B2B power as extra damage!

GAME MODES

ENDLESS: Play forever, aiming for the highest score.

ULTRA: Clear 40 lines as fast as possible.

SURVIVAL: Survive an endless, incoming rain of garbage blocks.

PUZZLES: Use the exact pieces given to fully clear the board.

VS BOT: Battle an AI opponent. Send them garbage lines to knock them out!

ENDURANCE: Fight bots back to back. Each wave the bot gets harder!
